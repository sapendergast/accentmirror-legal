## The service

AccentMirror is a pronunciation coaching app. It records what you say, scores your pronunciation, plays it back, and gives AI-generated coaching tips and practice phrases. It's provided by Stuart Pendergast, United Kingdom.

Scores, bands and tips are generated automatically and are for guidance only. Pronunciation assessment is inherently imperfect, and AI-generated content can occasionally be wrong. AccentMirror isn't a substitute for formal language instruction, and it doesn't promise any particular result.

## Your account and data

You don't need an account to use AccentMirror. The app creates an anonymous ID and backs up your practice history under it. Without an account, that history can't be restored after a reinstall or on another device. If you create an account in Settings, your history is linked to it and can be restored. See the Privacy Policy for details.

You're responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and for the phrases you choose to practise.

You can delete your account and cloud data at any time in **Settings → Delete Account**.

## Plans, trial and payments

- **Free trial:** new users get 7 days of full access, with up to 50 takes a day. No payment details are needed, and when the trial ends you move to Echo automatically.
- **Echo (free):** 10 takes a day, with ads. You can watch optional short video ads for up to 15 extra takes a day.
- **Fluent (paid):** monthly or annual subscription. Prices are shown in your local currency before you buy. Fluent includes unlimited daily takes under a fair-use policy. Normal use never comes close; the policy exists to stop automated abuse. If an account's use looks automated or unusually heavy, we may pause its takes for a while, and we'll tell you in the app when that happens.

Subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play and billed by them. They renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
